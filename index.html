From Ancient Snow to Modern Scoops

Did you know that ice cream has been enjoyed for over 2,000 years? 🍧 It started with flavored snow in ancient China and evolved into the creamy delight we love today!Ice cream originated from ancient frozen desserts in China, Persia, and India, evolving with the addition of milk, cream, and sugar in Europe. The development of modern ice cream is often credited to Italian cooks who added cream and sugar to the icy mixtures, and its popularity spread to France, England, and eventually America, where it was industrialized for mass production.
